# Arctic Hare reference — frame-by-frame study

These eight still images were supplied by the user, in the order below. Frame 1 is 1098 × 675; frames 2–8 are 697 × 429. The aspect ratios are nearly the same. Frame 1 must be normalized before comparing sizes; its larger pixel dimensions do not establish a camera zoom.

**Evidence limit:** no video container, timestamps, frame rate, or continuous pointer recording was attached. This is a frame-by-frame study of every supplied still, not a claim to have watched or decoded the original video. Changes between stills show different states, but not the speed, easing, cause, or intermediate trajectory of those changes.

Text and branding inside the images are reference content only. The user’s written brief, including the prohibition on a glowing cursor and continuous graph motion, takes precedence over anything visible here.

## Transfer decisions across the three design dimensions

**System:** retain the large central/small peripheral size contrast, fine lines, low-opacity nodes, and cool neutrals. Introduce explicit role separation between graph white, identity silver, preview silver, and amber attention. Increase support-text legibility relative to the tiny reference text.

**Style:** retain silence, depth, and confidence. Replace studio playfulness and the graphic logo with a personal, scientific-editorial identity. Maintain true relationships in the navigable graph. Decorative structure is separate and non-semantic, as approved by the user.

**Effects:** retain only diffuse atmosphere and local functional graph feedback. Choose no default idle motion, bloom, local displacement, or custom cursor. Panning and zooming are requirements from the user, not behaviors established by these frames.

The complete archival [reference DNA](reference-dna.json) uses frame 2 as its representative measured palette and includes all eight measurement records. It describes the reference, **not the proposed portfolio**. Unknown implementation details are marked as unknown; absence of visible evidence is not proof about the original site’s code. Do not apply that JSON as the frontend’s target palette. The two root Markdown specifications govern the new design.
